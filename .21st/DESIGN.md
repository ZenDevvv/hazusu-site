The display face is Dela Gothic One. Zen Kaku Gothic New carries supporting text. The [stylesheet](../src/site.css) defines the paper and ink colors, the photographic print treatment and the layouts for wide, narrow and short screens.

The cover, swipe guide, field notes, and closing edition share one continuous travelling Android device on desktop and tablet. Keep state transitions C¹-continuous with frame-rate-independent pose damping, scroll-scrubbed overlapping screen crossfades (no abrupt stops or binary snaps), and subtle 3D tilts that lean with gestures and angle toward companion copy (upright under reduced motion). Preserve the correspondence between a photo’s travel and its decision. The [site README](../README.md) records the release status, asset provenance and local preview commands.
